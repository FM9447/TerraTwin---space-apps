# Brag Plan: TerraTwin

## What is this app?
TerraTwin is an Earth–Moon–Mars Planetary Analog Discovery and Scientific Intelligence Workstation built for NASA International Space Apps Challenge 2026 (Challenge 9). It uncovers terrestrial locations that mirror permanent Moon base sites and Martian landscapes using 100% verified NASA DAAC and PDS scientific datasets.

## The angle
Before astronauts and rovers touch the regolith of Mars or the Lunar South Pole, billions of dollars of hardware and human survival systems are validated right here on Earth. TerraTwin calculates mathematical similarity between Earth and other worlds, matches field photos to their planetary twins, and challenges users in a 100-level visual perception game.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)

## Key moments (the middle)
- **Moment 1 (Reveal & Orbit HUD, 3-7s)**: "TerraTwin — Earth, Moon & Mars in Scientific Lock." Live starfield, telemetry badges, and the 3 celestial bodies.
- **Moment 2 (Demo: Inverse Parametric Discovery, 7-12s)**: Live NASA DAAC data sliders (NASADEM 30m, MODIS LST ΔT, ASTER GED basalt, GPM IMERG hyper-aridity, SEDAC remoteness) calculating Section 8 hybrid similarity ($S_{overall}$), coverage %, confidence %, and scientific breakdown.
- **Moment 3 (Demo: Visual Photo Twin Matcher, 12-16s)**: Field photo input $\rightarrow$ HUD scanning reticle $\rightarrow$ 92% morphological match to Valles Marineris (Mars) and Tycho Basin (Moon).
- **Moment 4 (Demo: 100-Level Campaign Game, 16-19s)**: Satellite terrain flashcard: "Is it Earth or Mars?" Answer revealed with streak multiplier.
